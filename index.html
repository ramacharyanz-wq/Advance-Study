Training first. Placement second.

Advance Study is a hospitality training and internship placement company based in Butwal, Nepal, founded in 2025.

Every year, we run practical skills events across Nepal covering bartending, barista craft, wine and food service, culinary arts, housekeeping, front office, and customer service, giving students a real platform to build their abilities. From there, we prepare candidates for the specific brand and role they're aiming for, and connect them with verified hospitality employers abroad.

What we offer

Skills training events

Year-round, hands-on workshops across every hospitality department, run throughout Nepal.

Global internship placement

Direct access to verified opportunities with hotels, resorts, and restaurants across eight countries.

Brand & role preparation

Training tailored to the specific brand standards and role you're placed into, before you ever meet the employer.

Visa & travel support

Guidance through your destination's visa process, so you can focus on preparing for the role ahead.
