Founder & Director

Ram Acharya

Ram founded Advance Study in 2025 to close the gap between hospitality training and what employers actually need. Rather than sending candidates abroad with a CV and good intentions, he built a program that trains people first, in the real systems and standards used on the job, from OPERA Hotel PMS and Micros POS to HACCP food safety practices, before they are ever introduced to an employer.

“ Candidates who arrive prepared build stronger, longer-lasting placements, for the student and the employer alike.

Based in Butwal, Ram works directly with hospitality employers across eight countries to understand exactly what each role requires, then shapes training around those specific standards. He is personally involved across every stage of Advance Study's work, from training design to visa guidance, and is always glad to hear directly from hospitality employers and prospective candidates.
